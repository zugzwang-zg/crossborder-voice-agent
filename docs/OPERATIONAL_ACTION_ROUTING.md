# Operational action-routing addendum

This addendum extends the stable insight decision contract only for packaging
and fulfillment candidates. It takes precedence over the base `action_type`
enumeration in `INSIGHT_METHODOLOGY.md` for the two categories below.

| category | action_type | recommendation source | accountable workstream |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `packaging_improvement` | `packaging` | `operations_recommendation` | Packaging and operations |
| `fulfillment_improvement` | `fulfillment` | `operations_recommendation` | Logistics and operations |

These routes keep product performance, packaging protection and order
fulfillment as separate hypotheses and owners. They do not establish the cause
of a review pattern. Each recommendation remains a validation task grounded in
the cited review evidence.
